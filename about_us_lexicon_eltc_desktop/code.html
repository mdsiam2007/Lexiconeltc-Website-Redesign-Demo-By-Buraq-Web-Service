Our Institutional Story

Redefining Language Pedagogy in South-Western Bangladesh

In 2014, Lexicon English Language Training Centre was established in Khulna to bridge a critical divide: regional students faced high barriers accessing standardized international English tests due to a lack of localized, certified testing infrastructure and structured communicative methodologies.

Moving decisively away from rote memorization and grammar-translation techniques, Lexicon introduced Canadian and Cambridge-aligned communicative language teaching (CLT). Over the last decade, we upgraded our campus to house Khulna's premier Computer-Delivered IELTS Hall, certified directly by the British Council.

Today, Lexicon ELTC stands as a comprehensive pathway provider. Through our global study-abroad arm, Lexifly, we extend the classroom experience directly into university admissions across Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the United States.
