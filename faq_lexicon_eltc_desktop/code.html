Testing Center & Exam Registration

Authorized British Council computer-delivered IELTS testing and Pearson PTE operations in Khulna.

As an officially recognized British Council venue, you can reserve your test date either on-site at our Shibbari More admissions desk or directly via our testing center portal:

  1. Submit your valid original passport details and preferred exam date.
  2. Choose your preferred speaking slot (often available on the exact same day).
  3. Complete payment via British Council official merchant link or at our counter.
  4. Receive your British Council verification code and test-day arrival confirmation notice.

Exam day security protocols are rigorously enforced under British Council guidelines:

  • Original Passport: The identical physical passport used during online registration. Photocopies or NID cards cannot be accepted.
  • Arrival Timing: Arrive at least 45 minutes prior to your scheduled station briefing for biometric scanning and photo capture.
  • Prohibited Items: Smartwatches, personal electronic devices, mechanical pencils, and opaque water bottles must be deposited in our secure electronic locker room. Transparent water bottles without labels are permitted.
