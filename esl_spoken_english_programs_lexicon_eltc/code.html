Measurable Milestones You Will Achieve in 90 Days

We measure real communicative autonomy, not memorized dialogues. By completing our syllabus, learners consistently pass these diagnostic checkpoints:

01

Cognitive Speech Reflexes

Eliminate internal translation from Bengali. Formulate ideas, respond to queries, and express opinions spontaneously within a 0.5-second natural latency interval.

Zero Latency Speaking
02

Accent & Intonation Neutrality

Targeted remediation of Regional Mother Tongue Influence (MTI). Master alveolar consonants (T, D, P), silent letters, elision, and natural sentence intonation.

Clear Neutral Dialect
03

1,500+ Active Collocations

Transition from simple words like "good" or "big" to nuanced professional expressions, idiomatic phrasal verbs, and situational frameworks used by native speakers.

Nuanced Word Wealth
04

Public Speaking Autonomy

Deliver 3-minute impromptu talks without shaking, stammering, or looking at notes. Confidently facilitate meetings, introduce guests, and defend viewpoints.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about admission, batch sizing, levels, and pedagogy at Lexicon Khulna.

You do not need prior fluency. However, you should have basic reading ability in English. We conduct a free 15-minute diagnostic assessment to determine whether you fit best in our Foundation group or our Intermediate/Advanced clinic. This ensures no student feels left behind or held back.
The assessment takes 15 minutes at our NLI Tower 7th floor center. It consists of a 5-minute computerized grammar/listening check followed by a friendly 10-minute conversational interview with one of our certified trainers (Farjana Jahan or Afrin Shruti). You receive an instant CEFR evaluation score (A1 to C1) with recommended batch tracks.
Regular classes follow a structured syllabus focusing on phonetics, syntax, lexical collocations, and error correction. The weekend Speaking Club (Fridays & Saturdays) is purely conversational and immersion-driven: open mic debates, peer discussions, and video analyses designed to build real-world stamina with zero academic pressure.
IELTS preparation focuses heavily on exam timing strategies, rubric criteria, and academic writing formats for candidates who already possess conversational English. ESL (English as a Second Language), on the other hand, builds foundational language instinct: pronunciation, vocabulary, sentence structuring, and confidence. For many candidates, 2 months of ESL is essential before taking an IELTS batch.
Yes. We run dedicated Friday & Saturday cohorts, as well as weekday night batches starting at 6:30 PM and 8:00 PM. These schedules are specially tailored for engineers, corporate bankers, physicians, and university faculty across Khulna.
